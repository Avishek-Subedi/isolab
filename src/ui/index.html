Validation against published laboratory data

The engine is semi-empirical. These are the constructions it was pinned against — if a physics change breaks one of these, it is a regression. Laboratory ratings are the ceiling, not the expectation: a wall tested at STC 55 routinely measures 45–50 as built, which is exactly why this simulator models leaks, ducts and flanking explicitly instead of quoting a lab number.
